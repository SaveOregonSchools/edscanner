Guided Search guide

Tell EdScanner what you want to find. The configured local Ollama model will propose a bounded search plan, while EdScanner validates every query and controls every district search.

This is an additional workflow. Search Websites and Search Profiles retain their full manual controls.

1. What are you trying to find?

Describe the evidence you want in ordinary language. You do not need to know EdScanner query syntax.

Examples

  • Contracts, program pages, or announcements involving Organization X.
  • DEI strategic plans that specifically discuss student affinity groups.
