How scheduling works

Each enabled schedule creates a normal monitoring sync for one board source at the selected local time. Weekly schedules run on the selected weekday. Monthly schedules use the selected day, or the month's final day when that date does not exist—for example, day 31 runs on April 30 and February 28 or 29.

Times shown here use the computer running EdScanner's local clock. Pausing a schedule does not delete its prior runs or collected records.
