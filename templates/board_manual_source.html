How manual sources are handled

Paste the public organization or meeting-list URL, not an administrator login. EdScanner safely retrieves the page and checks it with the selected platform adapter.

After the adapter verifies the endpoint and you confirm the district identity below, the match becomes the district's active working source. A safely retrieved link that cannot be verified is kept as manual_review evidence and cannot be scheduled or synced as a working source. Replacing a source preserves the earlier source below as history and carries its enabled monitoring schedule to the correction when possible.
